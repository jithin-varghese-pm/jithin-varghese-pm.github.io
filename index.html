Side project · AI product

Playref: a rules referee for road-trip games

Problem
Card games on road trips end in fights about rules nobody can check, and general AI chatbots confidently make rules up.
What I did
Took it from research to a live product: a phone-first UNO rules referee where hand-checked answers come first and AI is only the last step. Every AI reply must cite a real rule card, or the player sees "Not sure" instead of a guess.
Outcome
Live at $0 running cost, with a public accuracy score of 48 of 50 on a fixed test set, 41 of them answered with no AI at all.
